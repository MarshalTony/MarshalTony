# User manual

Sign in at **https://smis.bishopogez.com** with the username and temporary password you were given. You will be asked to choose your own password. Forgot it? Use *Forgot password* (needs an email on your account) or ask the school office. The system works on phones, tablets and computers.

---

## Subject teachers

* **Enter scores (Academics → Enter Scores).** Choose what to enter, then **1. Class → 2. Subject → 3. Term → 4. Year** (the current term and year are pre-selected). You only see the classes and subjects the system administrator has allocated to you; ask them if one is missing.
  * **Activity scores** (O-Level, competency-based): *Add chapter / theme* (applies to the whole class) → score each learner **out of 3** per chapter (e.g. 2.5; blank = not assessed) → add a short competency remark → *Save scores & remarks*. The average and identifier (3 Outstanding, 2 Moderate, 1 Basic) update as you type. After the term closes only the DOS can change scores.
  * **Exam scores:** choose the **Examination** (beginning of term, mid-term, end of term… — open exams are listed first and chosen automatically). Out of 100, or 80 for O-Level end of term. Type a mark, **ABS** (absent) or **EX** (exempted). Marks above the maximum are shown in red and cannot be saved. Press *Enter* to move down. *Save marks* as often as you like.
* **Other examinations** (beginning / mid term): Dashboard → *My classes & marks entry*, or Examinations → the exam → your subject and stream. The sheet works the same way.
* **Import marks from Excel:** *Download pre-filled template* (your students are already listed), fill the Mark column, upload, check the preview and confirm. Rows with errors are listed and not imported.
* **No approval step (default):** saved marks count straight away and appear on report cards; you can keep correcting them at any time — examinations stay open for marks entry. *If the school switches DOS approval on* (Settings), you instead *Submit marks* when every student has a mark/ABS/EX; they are then locked until the DOS approves or returns them with a reason.
* **Attendance:** Attendance → choose stream and date → tick P/A/L/E → *Save*.
* **Assignments & materials:** publish assignments (with due date and attachment) and learning materials for your classes; students see them in their portal.
* **Leave:** HR, Leave & Payroll → *Apply for leave*.

## Class teachers

Everything above, plus: write **class-teacher comments** and conduct for your stream (Examinations → exam → Report cards → *Comments*), record **discipline incidents**, and print your stream's report cards.

**Report card comments:** Examinations → the exam → *Comments* for your stream (switch streams at the top). Each student's **score and grade** are shown — their **average across all subjects** as printed on the report card (for O-Level end-of-term: subject totals out of 20 + EOT out of 80); suggested class-teacher and head-teacher comments follow this average. *Fill empty comments* writes a suggestion from the **Comment Bank** into every empty box (classmates get different wording; `{name}` becomes the first name); *↻* gives another suggestion for one student. Choose conduct from the list (Excellent … Poor) or type your own. Edit freely, then *Save* — only changed students are saved, and existing comments are never overwritten by *Fill*. Class teachers write the class-teacher column for their own stream; the headteacher and DOS their own columns.

**Comment Bank (Academics → Comment Bank):** the ready-made comments for class teachers, the headteacher and the DOS, each with a score range (e.g. 80–89.99 %). The headteacher, DOS and administrator can add, edit, switch off or delete comments.

## Director of Studies

**Entering scores for a teacher:** Academics → Enter Scores → *Activity scores* or *End of term scores* → **1. Teacher (staff) → 2. Class → 3. Subject → 4. Term → 5. Year**. Only that teacher's allocated classes and subjects are listed, and that teacher's name is printed as the subject teacher on the report card. (The system administrator and anyone with the "enter marks for any class" permission work the same way.)

Set up examinations, approve or return mark sheets, correct approved marks (with a reason), generate and publish results, view rankings (best 12 per class, best 3 per stream), subject analysis and broadsheets, write DOS comments, print report cards, manage timetables, the academic calendar and UNEB candidate records. See ADMIN_MANUAL.md → *Termly cycle*.

**Results analysis (Examinations → exam → Analysis, per class):** every student's score is their **average across all subjects** as on the report card (for O-Level end-of-term: each subject's total out of 20 + EOT out of 80). Shows the **overall winner**, **best and weakest subject**, **best stream**, **best 10 and last 10 in the class**, **best 10 and last 3 in each stream** (with class positions; ties share a position). Compared by default with the previous exam (change it, or choose *No comparison*). Shows candidates, class average and change, pass rate, boys vs girls, stream averages, grade spread; a **subject × stream table** with each subject teacher's name, colour-coded (green 70 %+, amber pass–69 %, red below the pass mark), the change since the comparison exam and boys/girls averages; **teacher averages** over the classes they teach; **most improved** and **biggest drops**; and **students at risk** (average below the pass mark, below the pass mark in N or more subjects, or a drop of N points — all adjustable). *PDF for staff meeting* prints the same in landscape.

**Report cards (Academics → Report Cards):** choose **1. Year → 2. Term → 3. Report** (the examination, e.g. End of Term), then **4. Class → 5. Stream** (or *All streams*) and *Generate report cards* — one PDF for the class or stream opens in a new tab. For a single student, type a name or admission number under **One student**, pick them and *Generate report card* (students who have left can still be found for past reports). Latest saved marks are included automatically; *Comments* and *Analysis* links open those pages for the chosen class.

## Bursar

* **Receive a payment:** School Fees → search the student → enter amount, method and the bank/MoMo/cheque reference → *Record payment & issue receipt* → *Print receipt*.
* **Mistake?** Use *Reverse* on the payment (with a reason) and record it again correctly. Payments are never edited or deleted.
* **Billing:** set fee structures and *Issue term invoices* at the start of term; ad-hoc charges via *Invoice* on a student's account.
* **Reports:** daily collections, payments register, outstanding balances (with guardian phone numbers), fees by class — PDF/Excel/CSV from Reports.
* **Accounts:** payment vouchers, other income, suppliers, purchase orders, petty cash, budgets, income & expenditure, cash flow.
* **Payroll:** HR → Payroll → *Prepare payroll* → adjust allowances/deductions → Headteacher approves → *Mark as paid*.

## Registrar

**Finding students (Students):** type in the search box — results update as you type. Every word must match, so `achieng moses` or `moses s1` work. It searches names, admission number, LIN, SchoolPay code, UNEB index, class code (S1–S6) and parents' names and phone numbers (`0772…` finds `+256772…`). Filter by class, stream (only that class's streams are listed), sex, day/boarding, **subject taken this year**, A-Level combination, **missing information** (no photo, LIN, SchoolPay code, parent or date of birth) and status. The line above the list shows the total, boys/girls and day/boarding; *Export Excel* exports exactly the filtered list. *Clear all filters* resets everything.

**Printing class lists (Students → Print list):** filter the list first (class, stream, subject, boarders, …), then *Print list* and choose a layout: **Nominal roll** (pick the columns — photo, adm. no., name, sex, class, day/boarding, date of birth, LIN, SchoolPay, parent, phone, subjects — plus optional blank columns such as *Fees, Uniform*; totals of boys and girls and a signature line), **Attendance / roll-call sheet** (landscape, blank boxes with optional headings such as *Mon, Tue…*), **Mark sheet** (blank columns, e.g. *Test 1, Test 2, Project*), **Parents' contact list**, or **Photo register**. The title is made from the filters unless you type one. Parents' phone numbers can be printed by the office, or by a class teacher for their own stream. Every print is recorded in the audit log.

**Leaving & clearance:** on a student's profile, *Leaving & clearance* shows five sign-offs — **Bursar** (fees balance), **Library** (books not returned, unpaid fines), **Stores** (items issued), **Discipline** (open cases) and **Class teacher**. Each office signs only its own line; if the system sees something outstanding (e.g. *Owes UGX 1,030,000*) a note is required to clear anyway. For a whole class (e.g. S4 at the end of the year) use **Students → Clearance**: every student with a tick per office, and one-click *Clear* where nothing is outstanding. The office then records the leaving (date, reason, conduct, remarks, next school): a certificate number is issued (e.g. LC-2026-0001), the status becomes Graduated / Transferred / Withdrawn / Expelled, this year's enrolment is closed and the portal login disabled. Documents (PDF, with letterhead, headteacher signature line and stamp box): **clearance form**, **leaving certificate / testimonial**, **transfer letter** and **recommendation letter** (also for current students). Letters and certificates can be printed by the office only; everything is in the audit log.

**Photos (Students → Photos):** choose a class (and optionally a stream; *Missing photos only* shows who still needs one) — each student appears as a card. Click a card to choose a photo (on a phone you can take it with the camera); it is resized and saved immediately. Hover a photo to change or remove it. For photo day, name each file with the admission number (e.g. `BOHS-2026-0021.jpg`) and use *Upload many*: each file goes to the matching student; unmatched file names are listed.

**Bulk edit (Students → Bulk edit):** choose a class (and optionally a stream) to edit all its students in one table — names, sex, stream, day/boarding, LIN, SchoolPay code, A-Level combination and **elective subjects** (one tick column per elective; click a subject heading to tick it for everyone, or for the learners you ticked). Tick learners and use *Apply to ticked* to set the stream, day/boarding or sex for many at once. Changed rows turn yellow; press *Save* to keep them or *Undo changes*. Stream changes also move the student's enrolment for the year; every change is in the audit log.

Register students (or import), print admission forms and ID cards, add parents/guardians and create parent portal accounts, record transfers and withdrawals, manage UNEB candidate records and transport allocations.

## Librarian, store keeper, nurse/counsellor, transport officer

* **Library:** register books (accession no., ISBN), *Issue* to a student (admission number) or staff, *Return* (overdue fine UGX 500/day, can be waived), see overdue books.
* **Inventory:** register items/assets, record stock received/issued/adjustments; the system refuses to issue more than is in stock and shows reorder alerts.
* **Welfare (confidential):** sick-bay visits and counselling notes are visible only to authorised welfare staff.
* **Transport:** vehicles, drivers, routes and fees, assign students (optionally adding the transport fee to their account), fuel and maintenance logs.

## Parents

After signing in you see **all your children**. Open a child to see:

* **School fees:** current balance, payments with downloadable receipts, and a full **statement** (PDF).
* **Results:** published examination results with grades and positions, and **report cards** to download — including earlier terms.
* **Attendance** this term, the **class timetable**, school **announcements** and **upcoming dates**.

You can only see your own children. If a child is missing, contact the school office. Change your password with the key icon at the top.

## Students

Your portal shows your details, results and report cards (once published), fees balance, attendance, class timetable, **assignments** and **learning materials** from your teachers, and school announcements. You can only see your own records.
