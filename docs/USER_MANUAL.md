# User manual

Sign in at **https://smis.bishopogez.com** with the username and temporary password you were given. You will be asked to choose your own password. Forgot it? Use *Forgot password* (needs an email on your account) or ask the school office. The system works on phones, tablets and computers.

---

## Subject teachers

* **Enter scores (Academics → Enter Scores).** Choose what to enter, then **1. Class → 2. Subject → 3. Term → 4. Year** (the current term and year are pre-selected). You only see the classes and subjects the system administrator has allocated to you; ask them if one is missing.
  * **Activity scores** (O-Level, competency-based): *Add chapter / theme* (applies to the whole class) → score each learner **out of 3** per chapter (e.g. 2.5; blank = not assessed) → add a short competency remark → *Save scores & remarks*. The average and identifier (3 Outstanding, 2 Moderate, 1 Basic) update as you type. After the term closes only the DOS can change scores.
  * **End of term scores:** the sheet for the end-of-term examination the DOS has opened for that class and term (out of 80 for O-Level). Type a mark, **ABS** (absent) or **EX** (exempted). Marks above the maximum are shown in red and cannot be saved. Press *Enter* to move down. *Save marks* as often as you like.
* **Other examinations** (beginning / mid term): Dashboard → *My classes & marks entry*, or Examinations → the exam → your subject and stream. The sheet works the same way.
* **Import marks from Excel:** *Download pre-filled template* (your students are already listed), fill the Mark column, upload, check the preview and confirm. Rows with errors are listed and not imported.
* **No approval step (default):** saved marks count straight away and appear on report cards; you can keep correcting them until the DOS closes the examination. *If the school switches DOS approval on* (Settings), you instead *Submit marks* when every student has a mark/ABS/EX; they are then locked until the DOS approves or returns them with a reason.
* **Attendance:** Attendance → choose stream and date → tick P/A/L/E → *Save*.
* **Assignments & materials:** publish assignments (with due date and attachment) and learning materials for your classes; students see them in their portal.
* **Leave:** HR, Leave & Payroll → *Apply for leave*.

## Class teachers

Everything above, plus: write **class-teacher comments** and conduct for your stream (Examinations → exam → Report cards → *Comments*), record **discipline incidents**, and print your stream's report cards.

## Director of Studies

**Entering scores for a teacher:** Academics → Enter Scores → *Activity scores* or *End of term scores* → **1. Teacher (staff) → 2. Class → 3. Subject → 4. Term → 5. Year**. Only that teacher's allocated classes and subjects are listed, and that teacher's name is printed as the subject teacher on the report card. (The system administrator and anyone with the "enter marks for any class" permission work the same way.)

Set up examinations, approve or return mark sheets, correct approved marks (with a reason), generate and publish results, view rankings (best 12 per class, best 3 per stream), subject analysis and broadsheets, write DOS comments, print report cards, manage timetables, the academic calendar and UNEB candidate records. See ADMIN_MANUAL.md → *Termly cycle*.

## Bursar

* **Receive a payment:** School Fees → search the student → enter amount, method and the bank/MoMo/cheque reference → *Record payment & issue receipt* → *Print receipt*.
* **Mistake?** Use *Reverse* on the payment (with a reason) and record it again correctly. Payments are never edited or deleted.
* **Billing:** set fee structures and *Issue term invoices* at the start of term; ad-hoc charges via *Invoice* on a student's account.
* **Reports:** daily collections, payments register, outstanding balances (with guardian phone numbers), fees by class — PDF/Excel/CSV from Reports.
* **Accounts:** payment vouchers, other income, suppliers, purchase orders, petty cash, budgets, income & expenditure, cash flow.
* **Payroll:** HR → Payroll → *Prepare payroll* → adjust allowances/deductions → Headteacher approves → *Mark as paid*.

## Registrar

Register students (or import), print admission forms and ID cards, add parents/guardians and create parent portal accounts, record transfers and withdrawals, manage UNEB candidate records and transport allocations.

## Librarian, store keeper, nurse/counsellor, transport officer

* **Library:** register books (accession no., ISBN), *Issue* to a student (admission number) or staff, *Return* (overdue fine UGX 500/day, can be waived), see overdue books.
* **Inventory:** register items/assets, record stock received/issued/adjustments; the system refuses to issue more than is in stock and shows reorder alerts.
* **Welfare (confidential):** sick-bay visits and counselling notes are visible only to authorised welfare staff.
* **Transport:** vehicles, drivers, routes and fees, assign students (optionally adding the transport fee to their account), fuel and maintenance logs.

## Parents

After signing in you see **all your children**. Open a child to see:

* **School fees:** current balance, payments with downloadable receipts, and a full **statement** (PDF).
* **Results:** published examination results with grades and positions, and **report cards** to download — including earlier terms.
* **Attendance** this term, the **class timetable**, school **announcements** and **upcoming dates**.

You can only see your own children. If a child is missing, contact the school office. Change your password with the key icon at the top.

## Students

Your portal shows your details, results and report cards (once published), fees balance, attendance, class timetable, **assignments** and **learning materials** from your teachers, and school announcements. You can only see your own records.
